About

A marketing agencybefore being atalent agency.

Aster is an independent influence marketing house. We connect brands with the right talent, come up with the creative ideas and run the entire execution — with a single point of contact, from brief to final report.

(01)Vision

Influence deserves the same rigour as any other marketing channel.

It has become a channel for acquisition, awareness and trust in its own right. Yet it is still too often bought by the post, without a clear objective or serious measurement. We believe the opposite: a plan, KPIs, a report — and creators chosen because they serve that plan.

(02)Approach

Three principles.

  1. 01

    Objectives first

    Every campaign starts from a measurable objective — sales, bookings, sign-ups, awareness — and how it will be measured.

  2. 02

    Few talents, well chosen

    We would rather work with five creators whose audience matches your target than fifty whose audience doesn’t.

  3. 03

    Everything is measured

    Tracked links and codes, KPIs agreed upfront, a final report set against the initial objectives.
