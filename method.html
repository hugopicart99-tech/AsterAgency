Our method

Five steps.No improvisation.

The method is the same for every client. What changes is what goes into it: your sector, your markets, your objectives.

01

01 — Brief

We start by understanding your brand, margins, markets, constraints and what you have already tried.

You receive

A brief summary and a first read of the objectives.

Indicative timing

One call and a questionnaire

02

02 — Strategy

Measurable objectives, target audiences, countries, key messages, formats, calendar and tracking KPIs.

You receive

A strategy document and a fixed fee.

Indicative timing

5 to 10 days

03

03 — Casting & connection

Talent selected on real audience, countries, language and each creator’s track record. We connect you with them or their representatives and handle every exchange.

You receive

A reasoned shortlist, profile by profile.

Indicative timing

3 to 7 days

04

04 — Creative & production

Creative ideas, concepts and scripts, talent briefs, shoot coordination, content approval and advertising disclosure compliance.

You receive

A publishing calendar and approved content before it goes live.

Indicative timing

According to the campaign calendar

05

05 — Measurement & report

Tracking of links, codes and KPIs during the campaign, then a final report set against the initial objectives.

You receive

A quantified report and recommendations for what comes next.

Indicative timing

During and after the campaign

(06)Measurement

What we track.

KPIs are chosen with you during the strategy phase, based on your objective. They are tracked throughout the campaign and consolidated in the final report.

  • Engagement rate
  • Clicks and traffic
  • Promo codes used
  • Tracked conversions
  • Cost per result

  • Content usage rights
